Privacy Policy – Digital Safe

Last updated: July 2026

Digital Safe is a personal document vault. Your documents, passwords and settings are stored locally on your device with AES-256 encryption. We do not upload your personal documents to any server.

Data stored on device

  • Encrypted documents, images and metadata
  • PIN hash and app settings
  • Points balance and shop purchases (local record)

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – for in-app purchases (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – we fetch https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N219.json to check IAP availability (no personal data sent)

Permissions

  • Camera / Photos – to scan and attach documents
  • Internet – IAP and remote config only
